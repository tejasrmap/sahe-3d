# Visual Regression & 3D Architectural Entity Verification Report

## 1. Scope of 3D Verification
In Phase 7.3.1, visual verification was executed across the southern entrance zone covering both the Cesium Architectural mode and Geographic satellite mode.

## 2. Key Visual Improvements
1. **Main Entrance Portal Architecture (`MainGatePortal.tsx`)**:
   - **Before (Phase 7.3)**: Flat elevated horizontal slab suspended above the ground with no pillars, or opaque solid extrusion blocking vehicular traffic.
   - **After (Phase 7.3.1)**: Distinct dual support pylons (West & East), overhead lintel arch spanning the 12m avenue, clear vehicle entrance opening (4.8m vertical clearance), university ceremonial signage truss, and limestone foundation plinths.
2. **Parking Canopies (`ParkingCanopy.tsx`)**:
   - **Before (Phase 7.3)**: Solid multi-story concrete block or floating roof slab without columns.
   - **After (Phase 7.3.1)**: Elevated corrugated tensile slate canopy roof ($3.4\text{ m}$ to $3.8\text{ m}$), 8 structural galvanized steel columns supporting the perimeter, with open ground clearance revealing parked two-wheelers and ground pavement.
3. **Bandar Road Corridor & Bus Stop**:
   - **Before (Phase 7.3)**: Bus stop pin placed on the westbound highway centerline in the middle of live traffic.
   - **After (Phase 7.3.1)**: Bus stop platform placed safely on the northern sidewalk apron, set back 2.52m from the live carriageway edge.
4. **Boundary Alignment**:
   - Perimeter glowing boundary line and translucent polygon highlight align exactly along the compound wall curb north of Bandar Road.
